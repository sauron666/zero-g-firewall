Originality check for the companion (14 Nov 15:25): the silhouette in one sentence is "a small glass ball holding a blue flame, circled by a tilted ring that carries two small lights". That sentence does not describe Clippy (a paperclip), a fire character with a face, a film robot, or the station's free-flyers (Astrobee is a cube; Int-Ball is a white ball with two large eyes on its body; CIMON is a ball with a screen face). The face is on the ring, the flame stays a flame. No NASA insignia. Drawn entirely by code written during the event; the pre-event concept preview was not opened or traced.

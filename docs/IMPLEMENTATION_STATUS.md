| Local, cloud and hybrid model modes of Ask the evidence | Connectors for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (which covers local servers such as Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp). On 14 November the team reported that a cloud model returned nothing. Three causes were found and fixed: the requests carried a sampling temperature, which current Claude and GPT models refuse with HTTP 400; the output limit of 700 tokens also covers a model's reasoning, so a reply could come back empty; and a greeting found no passages, so no model was called at all. Requests now follow each service's published rules of November 2026 (no temperature, a low effort or thinking level where the service knows one, room for 8,192 output tokens), and a request refused as malformed is followed by a plainer one. A "Test the connection" button sends one word and shows the service's own reply or error. Greetings, thanks, "who are you" and "what can you do" are answered by the companion itself in every mode. The conversation on every page now uses the connected model under the same rule as the Ask page: it may only reword what the search found, and the verifier has the last word. Request and reply formats, the fallback and the error messages are unit-tested with a stand-in for the network, and the whole path was driven in a headless browser against stand-ins for the three services. **No live model was called from the build environment, which has no key; the team tests against the live services.** |

## Known limits of what exists

- The Fan Paradox prescribes buoyancy as a plume fixed above the source; it does not travel with the smoke. Smoke units and the alarm level are relative; the ensemble varies them.
- Flame temperatures are equilibrium bounds for gaseous fuels. They say nothing about solid materials.
- The statement that BASS-II flames always went out when the fan stopped (ICES-2019-101) is not in the ledger. The site says only what the stored BASS-II Summary Report says: one test, flow cut, flame out within 2 s.
- The equilibrium check covers gas flames only. Nothing on the site predicts what a solid material does.
